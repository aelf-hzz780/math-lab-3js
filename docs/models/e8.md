# E8 · 八维晶格之心 / Heart of the E8 lattice

本实验展示经典 E8 晶格的 240 个最短向量。112 个整数根为 `(±1,±1,0⁶)` 的不同排列；128 个半整数根为负号数是偶数的 `(±½)⁸`。所有坐标是二进制可精确表示的整数或半整数，范数平方恰为 2。根之间内积为 1 时相连，即原八维距离平方为 2；共 6,720 条无向边，每点有 56 个邻居。

This experiment shows the 240 shortest vectors of the classical E8 lattice: 112 permutations of `(±1,±1,0⁶)` and 128 vectors `(±½)⁸` with an even number of minus signs. These dyadic coordinates are exact. Each squared norm is 2; an edge joins roots with inner product 1, giving squared distance 2. There are 6,720 undirected edges and 56 neighbors per root.

投影采用 seed 固定的 Gram–Schmidt 正交基；先在选定八维坐标平面做 Givens 旋转，再投影到三维。seed 只改变投影，不改变根系。画面统一放大 3 倍，球点半径为显示尺寸。低画质稀疏展示背景边，所选根的全部 56 条邻接仍完整显示。投影重叠不表示八维碰撞。

A seeded Gram–Schmidt orthonormal basis maps eight dimensions to three after a Givens rotation in the selected coordinate plane. The seed changes only the projection. Display coordinates have a uniform scale of 3; node radii are visual markers. Low quality samples background edges while retaining all 56 selected neighbors. Projected overlap does not imply an eight-dimensional collision.

E8 并非新发现。Viazovska 的最密填充证明首稿为 2016 年，2017 年发表于 Annals of Mathematics，相关工作获 2022 年 Fields Medal。这里只展示有限根系，不展示无限晶格、Fourier 最优函数或完整证明。

E8 is a classical structure. Viazovska's optimal packing proof first appeared in 2016 and was published in Annals of Mathematics in 2017; the work contributed to her 2022 Fields Medal. This is a finite root-system visualization, not the infinite lattice, the optimal Fourier function, or a proof reproduction.
